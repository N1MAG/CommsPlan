  <p class="section-kicker">A coordinated operating picture</p>
  <h2 class="home-heading">Radio context stays visible while you work.</h2>
  <p class="home-lead">CommsPlan brings together schedules, traffic, operational awareness, and the software assigned to each radio. It coordinates FLRig, FLDigi, FLMsg, FLAmp, JS8Call, VarAC, VARA, MeshCore, Meshtastic, and companion integrations such as JS8Spotter and CommStat while preserving those independently useful applications. Its built-in CommsPlan Spotter service manages station-wide watches, automation, and forms.</p>
