    <p class="section-kicker">Where and when</p>
    <h2 id="a-plan-you-can-use" class="home-heading">Make the plan part of your day.</h2>
    <p class="home-lead">Build a frequency plan from your daily schedule, nets, and standard operating procedures. Assign it to a radio and review how the pieces fit together. When plans change, you can see which operating windows are affected.</p>
    <figure class="product-frame">
      <a href="./plan-builder.png" aria-label="Open full-size plan builder screenshot"><img src="/plan-builder.png" alt="CommsPlan Plan Builder demonstration showing how daily schedules, nets, and operating procedures combine into a frequency plan" width="1450" height="1000" loading="lazy"></a>
      <figcaption>Plan Builder brings daily schedules, nets, and operating procedures into one plan you can review.</figcaption>
    </figure>
    <p class="section-link"><a href="./guide/operating-picture#plans-and-scheduling">Explore plans and scheduling →</a></p>
    <div class="home-assurance">
      <h3>You stay in control.</h3>
      <p>Assign a reviewed Frequency Plan to follow its schedule. Holds, busy checks, and manual controls help you manage changes while you operate.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

    <p class="section-kicker">Comms Unity</p>
    <h2 id="comms-unity" class="home-heading">Bring the pieces together.</h2>
    <p class="home-lead">Your plans, radios, applications, and traffic all have a part to play. CommsPlan connects them in one workspace, so you can move from the schedule to a message to the map without losing track of the task.</p>
    <figure class="product-frame">
      <a href="./operational-map.png" aria-label="Open full-size operational map screenshot"><img src="/operational-map.png" alt="CommsPlan offline map demonstration showing sample stations and regional information alongside map controls" width="1450" height="1000" loading="lazy"></a>
