**CommsPlan makes your comms plan work.** Know where to be, when to be there,
and what to do when you’re there. Bring your plans, radio tools, and message
intelligence together in one workspace, whether you use one radio or several.

New here? Start with the three questions below, then choose the setup path that
fits your station. The [GitHub Releases page](https://github.com/N1MAG/CommsPlan/releases/latest)
has the latest downloads and release notes.

## Where, when, and what

- **Where to be:** Choose the frequency, mode, and radio for the group you want to reach.
- **When to be there:** Follow daily schedules and nets, and see what comes next.
- **What to do when you’re there:** Review traffic, prepare a message, use a form, or follow your operating procedure.

The Station Control Bar keeps your radio and schedule in view while you work.
Message Intelligence helps you find useful information in the traffic, with the
original messages available when you need the details.

The Station Setup checklist and workflow described below are part of CommsPlan
2.0.6. Use the installation and upgrade guide to select a currently published
package or source installation.

## Before you begin

- Know which computer and operating system will run CommsPlan.
- Know where an existing source checkout was installed, if you use one.
- Close CommsPlan and affected companion applications before an upgrade.
- Keep the radio manufacturer and model available for the guided setup.
- Treat a verified station backup as mandatory for an upgrade.

::: tip One radio is enough
You do not need to add another radio to use CommsPlan. One-radio operation remains fully supported.
:::

## First successful session

The recommended order is:

1. Open **Configuration → Main → Station setup** and review the saved checklist.
2. Enter your operator details and the groups you work with.
3. Choose a radio and review its software, connections, operating choices, and schedule.
4. Review the saved setup summary. Scheduling stays off until you explicitly review and enable it.
5. Open Ops Center, Messages, and Map to get to know your station’s workspace.

<div class="expectation">
  <strong>Expected result</strong>
  Your station details are saved, your radio tools are configured, and you know where to review the radio’s schedule.
</div>

## Bring it together

Build a plan, assign it to your radio, and review your operating windows in
Ops Center. Then use Messages and Map to understand incoming traffic and decide
what to do next. Your familiar radio applications keep their own controls.
